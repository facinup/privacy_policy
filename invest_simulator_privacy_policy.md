# Privacy Policy for Invest Simulator
**Last updated: September 2026**

## Introduction
Invest Simulator (“the App”) is a simple decoder for flashing light, e.g. Morse code. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data.

## No Data Collection
The App does **not** collect, store, process, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.  
Specifically, the App does **not** collect:

- Names  
- Email addresses  
- Phone numbers  
- Device identifiers  
- Location data  
- Usage analytics  
- Cookies  
- IP addresses  
- Search history  
- Browsing history outside Archive.org  

## No Accounts or Login
The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, or authenticate in any way.

## No Third‑Party Services
The App does not integrate with:

- Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)  
- Advertising networks  
- Social media SDKs  
- Crash reporting tools  
- Tracking libraries  

## Children’s Privacy
The App is suitable for all ages and does not collect any personal information from children.  
Parents or guardians using Family Link may approve installation of the App.

## Changes to This Policy
If the App’s functionality changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.  
The updated version will always be available at this URL.
