# Privacy Policy for Archive Org Browser
**Last updated: July 2026**

## Introduction
Archive Org Browser (“the App”) is a simple web-view application that allows users to browse publicly available content hosted on Archive.org. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data.

## No Data Collection
The App does **not** collect, store, process, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.  
Specifically, the App does **not** collect:

- Names  
- Email addresses  
- Phone numbers  
- Device identifiers  
- Location data  
- Usage analytics  
- Cookies  
- IP addresses  
- Search history  
- Browsing history outside Archive.org  

## No Accounts or Login
The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, or authenticate in any way.

## No Third‑Party Services
The App does not integrate with:

- Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)  
- Advertising networks  
- Social media SDKs  
- Crash reporting tools  
- Tracking libraries  

## Internet Access
The App connects directly to Archive.org to display publicly available content.  
All interactions occur between the user and Archive.org.  
The App does not intercept, store, or analyze any data exchanged with Archive.org.

Please refer to Archive.org’s own privacy policy for information about how Archive.org handles user data.

## Children’s Privacy
The App is suitable for all ages and does not collect any personal information from children.  
Parents or guardians using Family Link may approve installation of the App.

## Changes to This Policy
If the App’s functionality changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.  
The updated version will always be available at this URL.
